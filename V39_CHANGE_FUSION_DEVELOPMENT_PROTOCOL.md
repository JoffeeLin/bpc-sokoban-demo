# BPC v0.39 typed raw-change fusion development protocol

After a separate-task episode succeeds, every raw transition in that same trace
is replayed into the discovered anonymous factor's existing Beta change cube.
The candidate multiplies the v0.38 event-boundary action distribution by the
factor-balanced posterior `P(raw frame changes | current raw relations, action)`.
This is an unweighted probability product, not a collision rule or reward.

Development uses 24 independent-generator worlds at seed `239110`, excluding
training initials, the v0.33 frozen holdout, and reproducible v0.34–v0.38 suites.
Evaluation seed is `239310`, with 32 episodes per world and 48 steps. Old
retention uses generation seed `239210`, evaluation seed `239410`, 16 episodes
per world, and 128 steps.

Before results, adoption requires all of:

- at least 75% aggregate success and four successes on every world;
- at least five percentage points over v0.38 boundary control, the prior v7
  fixed-0.24 fusion, global action change rates, shared change probabilities,
  rotated change columns, and change-only control;
- at least ten percentage points over deletion of every old factor;
- at least thirty percentage points over uniform and rotated semantics;
- at least 95% of field success on every old family;
- identical old training-event counts, zero train-world object/mark overlap,
  and zero evaluation writes/digest changes.

classifier.dev routed the factor-specific typed product at `0.82` confidence
and marked exact v7 0.24 fusion as known weak at `0.98`. It is development
routing only. Failure does not change gates.
